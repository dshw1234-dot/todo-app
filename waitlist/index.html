Three things it does first

  1. Capture it the moment it comes up

    Add a to-do from your phone in seconds. No need to hold it in your head until you're back at your desk.

  2. Hand it off in one line

    No new chat to open, no folder or model to pick, no long prompt to rewrite. The to-do you wrote is the brief.

  3. Progress and results, in the task

    Each task shows where the work stands, and the result comes back to the same task. No digging through chat history.

From to-do to done, in one place

  1. Write it downAdd to-dos from your phone or PC, the way you would in any to-do app.
  2. Hand it offFor work you'd give to AI, switch the task to Checkbot and request a run.
  3. Your AI does the workCheckbot passes the task to the AI app on your PC. The actual work is done by the AI you already use.
  4. Review and check it offWhen the result comes back to the task, read it and tick it off. Not quite right? Add a note and hand it off again.

Questions

Can't I do this with the Claude or ChatGPT app?

You can. But you copy the task over, explain it again, and later dig the result out of a chat. The AI does the work; Checkbot keeps track of it. It organizes your to-dos, hands them to your AI, shows where each one stands, and collects the result in the task.

Do I pay Checkbot for AI usage?

No. Checkbot runs the AI you already use and doesn't resell AI. Checkbot's own pricing isn't set yet; the to-do list features are planned to be free.

What do I need?

For now, a Windows PC and your phone. Checkbot passes tasks to the AI app installed on your PC, so tasks run while the PC is on. From your phone (iPhone or Android) you add tasks, hand them off, and read the results.

When can I try it?

Early-access invites go out by email in the order people joined the waitlist.
